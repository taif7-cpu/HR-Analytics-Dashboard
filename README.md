# HR-Analytics-Dashboard

An HR Analytics Dashboard built with Power BI to analyze workforce data, employee performance, and employee attrition.

## Project Overview

This project provides an interactive dashboard for exploring key HR metrics and identifying patterns related to employee demographics, performance, satisfaction, and attrition.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. HR Dashboard - Overview

Provides an overall view of the workforce, including:
- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Terminated Employees
- Average Salary
- Average Age
- Employees by Department
- Employees by Sex
- Employment Status
- Performance Score

### 2. Workforce Profile
Analyzes employee characteristics and workforce composition, including:
- Average Years of Service
- Average Satisfaction
- Total Absences
- Marital Status
- Citizenship Status
- Race
- Years of Service
- Performance Rating
- Employee demographics by department and age

### 3. Attrition Analysis
Focuses on employee turnover and attrition patterns, including:
- Attrition Rate
- Terminated Employees
- Average Salary of Terminated Employees
- Average Age of Terminated Employees
- Termination Reasons
- Attrition Rate by Department
- Terminated Employees by Department
- Terminated Employees by Position
- Terminated Employees by Performance Rating
- Employee Engagement and Satisfaction
